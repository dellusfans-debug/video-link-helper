About Video Link Helper

Simple tools and helpful video guides

Who We Are

Video Link Helper is an independent website project designed to provide simple online tools and educational guides about video links and responsible use of online media.

What This Website Does

Our current version provides a basic tool to check the format of a URL. It does not fetch, process, or download videos.

Responsible Use

Users should respect copyright, privacy, and the terms of the platforms where videos are hosted. Use content only when you own it or have the necessary permission.

Independent Website

This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Facebook, Meta, or other video platforms.
